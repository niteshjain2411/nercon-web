NERCON CaseQuest 2026

Recognising Challenging Diagnostic Cases by Faculty from North East India

About CaseQuest

CaseQuest is an exclusive academic session inviting faculty from North East India to present diagnostic pathology cases that challenge, educate and inspire. The session aims to showcase genuine diagnostic dilemmas with important learning points.

Eligibility Criteria

  • 1Only one case per institute is permitted.
  • 2Cases must represent genuine diagnostic challenges and be adequately worked up to justify the final diagnosis.
  • 3Only Faculty members are eligible to submit and present; residents/others are not eligible.
  • 4The submitting Faculty and presenting Faculty must be the same individual.
  • 5Submitting Faculty must be a valid NERC-IAPM member. If the faculty is a non-member and wishes to submit and present, then he/she should apply for NERC-IAPM membership in advance and at the earliest.
  • 6NERCON 2026 registration is mandatory for the submitting Faculty.
  • 7Submission deadline: 15 September 2026

Selection Process

  • The Organising Committee will endeavour to accommodate all eligible submissions.
  • However, if the number of submissions exceeds the available presentation slots, all the cases will be anonymized and assigned to expert pathologists outside North East India by the Organising Committee for independent review.
  • Selection will be based solely on reviewer scores.
  • The decision of the Organising Committee shall be final and binding, with no provision for reconsideration.

Submission Protocol

  • 1Email complete case details, relevant images and supporting material to papernercon2026@gmail.com
  • 2Case files/details must not contain author or institute identifiers.
  • 3Submissions by any other mode will not be accepted.
  • 4Email subject should be:
    "Interesting Case – Delegate ID"  (e.g. Interesting Case – ID No. 12345)

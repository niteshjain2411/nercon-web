PAPER PUBLICATION AWARD

NERCON 2026 — Recognising Excellence in Pathology Research

About the Award

A hallmark of academic distinction, the Paper Publication Award at NERCON 2026 is instituted to recognize and honour exemplary original research published by contributors from the North Eastern region. This award underscores scientific rigor, originality, and meaningful contribution to the discipline of pathology.

Eligibility Criteria

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
  1. Consideration is limited to original research articles published in indexed journals. Case reports, case series, and review articles are not eligible.
  2. The research must have been conducted within the North Eastern region.
  3. Submission is restricted to the first author or corresponding author.
  4. The submitting author must hold valid membership of NERC-IAPM. Non-members are advised to obtain membership at the earliest.
  5. NERCON 2026 registration is mandatory for all applicants.
  6. Eligible publications must fall within the period: 1 September 2025 – 29 August 2026
  7. Submission Deadline: 30 August 2026

Submission Protocol

SUBMISSION PROTOCOL
  1. A soft copy of the complete published article must be submitted via email to:
    papernercon2026@gmail.com
  2. Submissions through any alternate mode will not be entertained.
  3. The email subject line must strictly follow the format:
    Paper Award – Delegate ID
    (Example: Paper Award – ID No. 12345)

Author Details

AUTHOR DETAILS (MANDATORY IN EMAIL BODY)
